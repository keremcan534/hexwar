# Görsel üretim listesi

Dosyaları bu klasöre (`assets/incoming/`) bırak, dilimleyip oyuna bağlamak bende.
Her istemi ChatGPT'ye olduğu gibi yapıştır. Sayfalar eski setle aynı biçimde
olsun ki hepsi aynı filmden çıkmış gibi dursun.

## Ortak stil (her isteme ekle)

> Photorealistic period photograph, 1900-1945 Europe, shot on a vintage large
> format camera, muted desaturated steel-blue and grey palette, soft overcast
> light, fine film grain, natural skin and fabric texture, no text, no logos, no
> watermark, no modern objects, not illustrated, not painterly.

## 1. Eksik gösterge kartları (Trade ekranı): 1 sayfa (tamamlandı, oyunda)

Dosya adı: `kpi/kartlar-ticaret.webp`

> One image, 1536×1024, a 2 columns × 3 rows contact sheet of six separate wide
> photographs, separated by solid pure magenta (#FF00FF) gutters 20 px wide,
> including an outer magenta border. In every photograph the LEFT 40% is empty
> dark fog or plain wall, and the subject stands in the RIGHT 60%.
> Row 1 left: dock workers loading crates onto a steam freighter (exports).
> Row 1 right: customs officers inspecting foreign cargo on a rainy quay (imports).
> Row 2 left: a clerk at brass merchant scales weighing gold against ledgers (trade balance).
> Row 2 right: a long queue of people at an empty bakery shelf (shortages).
> Row 3 left: grey warships blocking a harbour mouth at dusk (blockade).
> Row 3 right: rows of clerks at telegraph machines in a ministry hall (automation).

## 2. General portreleri: 4 sayfa, 200 yeni yüz (tamamlandı, oyunda)

Oyunda 50 yüz var (32 kara, 18 deniz), kalabalık dünyada tekrar ediyorlar. Her
sayfa 50 yüz getirir. Aynı istemi yalnız kıyafet satırını değiştirerek dört kez
kullan.

Dosya adları: `subay-kara-1.webp`, `subay-kara-2.webp`, `subay-kara-3.webp`,
`subay-deniz-1.webp`

> One image, 2000×1000, a strict grid of 10 columns × 5 rows of 50 different
> head-and-shoulders military ID portraits, each cell the same size, no gutters
> and no captions. Every man is a clearly different individual: vary age from 35
> to 70, face shape, nose, ears, skin tone, hair, baldness, moustache, beard and
> scars, and include faces from Europe, the Middle East, Central Asia, East Asia,
> South Asia, Africa and Latin America. Same neutral grey studio backdrop, same
> soft window light from the left, looking at the camera, serious expression.
>
> Sayfa 1-3 kıyafet: army officers in varied 1900-1945 field uniforms, high
> collars, peaked caps, kepis, kalpaks and turbans, with rank insignia and medals.
>
> Sayfa 4 kıyafet: naval officers in dark and white 1900-1945 uniforms, peaked
> caps with anchors, gold sleeve rank and medals.

## 4. Gazete fotoğrafları: 4 sayfa, 24 haber karesi

Gazete şimdilik gösterge ve menü fotoğraflarını kullanıyor. Şu haberlerin karşılığı
yok: başkentin düşüşü, devrim, teslim, açlık kuyruğu, borsa paniği, salgın, deniz
batışı. Oyun kareyi siyah beyaza ve gazete tramına kendisi çevirir, bu yüzden
renkli üretmen yeterli.

Bu sayfada ortak stilin renk satırı yerine şunu kullan:

> Photorealistic press photograph, 1900-1945, taken on the spot with a hand-held
> plate camera, natural documentary framing, people caught mid-action and not posing,
> soft overcast light, deep focus, fine film grain, natural skin and fabric texture,
> no text, no readable signs, no flags with real national symbols, no logos, no
> watermark, no modern objects, not illustrated, not painterly.

Sayfa biçimi dördünde de aynı:

> One image, 1536×1024, a 2 columns × 3 rows contact sheet of six separate wide
> photographs, separated by solid pure magenta (#FF00FF) gutters 20 px wide,

> the subject filling the frame.

## 5. Lider portreleri: 5 sayfa, 250 yüz

Her ülkenin başında adı, yüzü ve kişiliği olan bir lider var. Portre gelene dek
erkek liderler subay vesikalıklarını taşıyor, kadın liderlerde yalnız baş harfler
görünüyor. Sayfalar general sayfalarıyla aynı biçimde: 10 sütun × 5 satır, hücre
sırası soldan sağa, yukarıdan aşağıya. Oyun fotoğrafa sepya baskıyı kendi uygular,
sen renkli ya da gri üretebilirsin.

Dosya adları: `lider-hukumdar.webp`, `lider-devlet-1.webp`, `lider-devlet-2.webp`,
`lider-asker.webp`, `lider-devrimci.webp`

Aynı istemi yalnız kıyafet satırını değiştirerek beş kez kullan:

> One image, 2000×1000, a strict grid of 10 columns × 5 rows of 50 different
> head-and-shoulders formal studio portraits, each cell the same size, no gutters
> and no captions. Every person is an invented individual who does not resemble any
> real historical figure, and each is clearly different from the others: vary face
> shape, nose, ears, skin tone, hair, baldness, moustache, beard and spectacles, and
> include faces from Europe, the Middle East, Central Asia, East Asia, South Asia,
> Africa and Latin America. Same neutral grey studio backdrop, same soft window
> light from the left, head centred in the upper half of the cell, shoulders square
> to the camera, composed and dignified expression.
>
> `lider-hukumdar` kıyafet: Rows 1 to 4 are reigning kings, emperors, princes, khans
> and emirs aged 25 to 70, in ceremonial court dress with sashes, orders, epaulettes,
> fur collars and robes of state, some in jewelled turbans or kalpaks. Row 5 is ten
> reigning queens and empresses aged 25 to 70, in high-collared court gowns with
> pearls, tiaras and an order on a sash.
>
> `lider-devlet-1` kıyafet: civilian prime ministers and presidents of 1900-1918,
> men aged 45 to 75, in black frock coats, wing collars and cravats, with watch
> chains, pince-nez, full beards and waxed moustaches, a few in a fez or turban.
>
> `lider-devlet-2` kıyafet: civilian prime ministers and presidents of 1919-1945,
> men aged 45 to 75, in dark double-breasted lounge suits, soft collars and striped
> ties, with round spectacles, clipped moustaches or clean-shaven, hair brushed back.
>
> `lider-asker` kıyafet: marshals and heads of military juntas, men aged 45 to 68, in
> heavy dress uniforms with gold braid, high collars, rows of medals and peaked caps
> or kepis, hard and unsmiling.
>
> `lider-devrimci` kıyafet: Rows 1 to 4 are revolutionary party chairmen aged 30 to
> 58, in plain dark tunics buttoned to the neck, leather coats, worn jackets and
> workers' caps, unshaven, with an intense stare. Row 5 is ten women revolutionary
> leaders aged 30 to 55, in plain dark dresses or tunics with their hair pinned back.
